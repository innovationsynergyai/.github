# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for security problems.

Email **michael@innovationsynergyai.com** with the subject line `SECURITY` and include:

- The repository, app, or endpoint affected
- The version or build number, and the OS version
- Steps to reproduce
- What an attacker could achieve
- Any logs or proof of concept, with customer data removed

You will get an acknowledgement within **3 business days**. Confirmed issues get a remediation plan within **10 business days**, and shipped apps get an expedited App Store submission where the severity warrants it.

## Scope

In scope: source in public repositories under this account, and shipped iOS, iPadOS, and macOS applications published by Innovation Synergy AI Inc.

Out of scope: third-party services and SDKs (report those to their vendors), social engineering, physical access attacks, denial of service through volume alone, and findings from automated scanners with no demonstrated impact.

## Client engagements

If the finding affects a client deployment, report it to the address above and do not contact the client directly. Coordinated disclosure is handled through Innovation Synergy AI so the client can be notified and patched at the same time.

## Handling of data

Do not exfiltrate, retain, or share customer data while testing. Use your own test accounts. Stop and report as soon as you can demonstrate impact.

## Credit

Good-faith reports are credited in release notes unless you ask to stay anonymous. There is no paid bounty program at this time.
