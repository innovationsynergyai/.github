# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

These projects are built alongside working businesses — contractors, technicians, dispatchers, and office staff. The same standard applies here as on a job site: be direct, be useful, and treat people with respect.

Expected:

- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person
- Assume competence and ask before assuming intent
- Respect that people contribute across time zones and around day jobs
- Keep discussion in the issue or pull request where it belongs

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Posting client names, customer records, credentials, or engagement details in public
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews

## Scope

Applies in all repositories under this account, in issues, pull requests, discussions, and commits, and anywhere someone is representing these projects in public.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **michael@innovationsynergyai.com**. Reports are handled privately and the reporter is not identified without consent.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, and block accounts. There is one maintainer here, so decisions are made directly and without appeal committee theater — but they will be explained if you ask.
