# Support

## Open source repositories

Open an issue on the repository in question and use one of the templates. Include the OS version, device, app or package version, and steps to reproduce. Issues without reproduction steps usually get closed.

This is not a staffed support desk. Public repositories here are maintained around client work, so response times vary.

## Shipped apps

If you are an end user of an app built by Innovation Synergy AI — for example [East Coast Air](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/east-coast-air/id6770141635) — contact the business that operates the app first. They handle scheduling, quotes, memberships, and billing.

For bugs and crashes in the app itself, email **michael@innovationsynergyai.com** with the app name, your device model, and the iOS version.

## Existing clients
